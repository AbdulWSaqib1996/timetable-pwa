# Gap audit after V0–V4 — 10 September 2026

**Why:** after V4 shipped, the owner reported that not every change the visual audit asked for was visible ("settings once you drill into them are unchanged, the map detail view is unchanged"). This is a full check of every screen and sheet against `VISUAL_UI_AUDIT.md` §3–§15 and the V0–V4 batch definitions, with the evidence method and the fixes (Pass 62).

## Method

1. A scripted sweep of the built app captured 68 screens at 390px and 1280/1440px — every primary route, every Settings sub-page (top and scrolled), the session detail (overview, scrolled, travel), journey home, and every sheet reachable from those pages (changes, filters, plan week, task editor, evidence journal, PGCE file on Overview/Targets/Wallet, term stats, course setup, study group, placement page), plus all six admin pages and the lock screen at both widths.
2. The same sweep ran the V1 measurement walk on each capture: controls under 44px, text under 14px outside the named metadata classes, and emoji in controls or labels.
3. Each capture was compared with the audit's "Proposed / Implementation / Acceptance" text and the corresponding `designs/after-*.png`.

**Deployment note:** the live main bundle (`main-BDmp5K7C.js` at the time of the check) already contained the V3 travel panel (`travel-od`), and the Settings chunk contained the tiled index. A device that had the app open before a release keeps the previous service-worker build until the app is closed and reopened (the update applies "on the next open", as Help says), so a freshly opened app shows the current build. The gaps below are real regardless of that.
